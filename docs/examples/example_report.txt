INTERPRETATION NOTES FOR ADVISOR:

1. PRIORITY SCORES: Higher scores indicate more important/confident rules.
   Rules are sorted by priority, so the top rules are the most critical
   decision patterns learned by the agent.

2. BOARD STATES: Each rule activates under specific board conditions.
   - X = Player's piece (the agent)
   - O = Opponent's piece
   - . = Empty position (confirmed)
   - ? = Unconstrained (not checked by this rule)
   - ?/X = Not opponent (could be empty or player)
   - ?/O = Not player (could be empty or opponent)

3. ACTION PRIORITIES: For each board state, the model assigns scores to
   all possible actions. These are converted to probabilities via softmax.
   The "BEST" action is the one with highest score.

4. SUPPORT COUNT: Number of training samples that matched this rule.
   Higher support means the rule was encountered more frequently during
   training, making it more reliable.

5. POLICY VERIFICATION: This explicit rule representation allows us to:
   - Verify the policy makes sense strategically
   - Check for potential bugs or incorrect learning
   - Understand the agent's decision-making process
   - Compare against optimal strategies (like MinMax)
